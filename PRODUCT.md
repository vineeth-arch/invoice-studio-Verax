# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders, studio owners, and freelancers at boutique creative service businesses in India (primary: Design Innsaeit, Mumbai). They invoice clients on project completion, track GST liabilities, and manage purchase orders with vendors. Context: desktop, office or studio environment, deliberate use — not glance-and-go. They value craft and are aesthetically literate; a beautiful tool is a point of pride, not a nice-to-have.

## Product Purpose

VeRAX (GST Invoice Studio) is a GST-compliant invoicing and financial operations tool for Indian service businesses. It generates professional tax invoices, tracks payment status, manages purchase orders, and surfaces financial KPIs. Success looks like: every invoice issued in under 90 seconds, zero GST calculation errors, and a dashboard that makes financial health legible at a glance without needing a spreadsheet.

## Brand Personality

Precision instrument. Analogue craft. Quiet authority.

The product feels like a premium analogue accounting machine from the 1970s placed on a modern desk — tactile, physical, trustworthy. Not startup-SaaS. Not enterprise-cold. The design language is "Physical Depth": neumorphic surfaces (single base colour #dde3ea, depth via shadow only) combined with skeuomorphic controls (analog gauges, rotary knobs, LED displays, physical toggles) that behave like real instruments.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (Freshbooks, Wave, Zoho Books) — white cards, blue gradients, flat icon grids
- Purple/violet invoice tools — the accent is deep instrument steel (#1c3d5a), never purple
- Flat/Material Design — no border-based separation, no flat buttons, no elevation via colour change
- Bootstrap/Tailwind generic components — pill buttons, shadcn dialog patterns, etc.
- Chart.js / Recharts visual language — no generic rounded bars, no tooltip bubbles on pastel backgrounds

## Design Principles

1. **Every surface has a z-position.** Raised or pressed — nothing is truly flat. Shadow IS the border, the card, the hierarchy.
2. **Controls must feel holdable.** Knobs rotate. Toggles slide with spring physics. Gauges have needles that could snap off. The user should want to touch the screen.
3. **Instruments over dashboards.** Financial data presented as analogue instrument readings, not SaaS metric tiles. Numbers live in LED enclosures, not hero cards.
4. **One material, infinite depth.** The entire surface is #dde3ea. Variety comes from shadow geometry alone — never from colour fills, borders, or gradient backgrounds.
5. **Indian-first numerals.** All currency in INR with en-IN locale (1,00,000 not 100,000). GST at 18%. Rupee symbol always present.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Keyboard navigable controls (toggles respond to Space/Enter). Focus indicators required on all interactive elements. Colour is never the sole differentiator for status — text labels and shape accompany all status colours.
